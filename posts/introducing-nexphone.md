If you run a service business, your phone is your storefront — and it's unattended most of the day.
You're under a sink, on a roof, with a client. The call goes to voicemail, the caller doesn't leave one
(most don't), and they dial the next name in the search results.

NexPhone is a business phone system built around that exact moment.

## What you get

**A real business number.** Local or toll-free, live in minutes. Calls, texts, voicemail — separate from
your personal phone, shared with your team if you have one.

**An AI receptionist that actually answers.** Not a menu maze — a natural voice that picks up when you
can't, answers questions from your business info, takes down the caller's name, number, and what they
need, and knows the difference between a real job and a wrong number.

**The lead lands in your pocket.** A hot lead texts you immediately with what the caller wanted and how
to reach them back. Everything else waits politely in your follow-up list.

**Missed-call text-back.** When a call slips through, the caller instantly gets a text — "sorry we missed
you, how can we help?" — which keeps the conversation alive instead of losing it to the next listing.

## Built for how small businesses actually work

- Set up in one sitting: pick a number, tell us about your business, done.
- Your availability drives everything — on-shift you ring, off-shift the AI covers.
- Add teammates when you grow; share lines without sharing your personal number.
- Honest pricing, no per-minute surprises. See [pricing](/pricing).
