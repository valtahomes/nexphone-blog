July was about one theme: making US texting registration — the part of business texting nobody enjoys —
as painless as it can honestly be.

## Guided consent setup, no essay required

Carriers reject registrations whose consent story they can't verify, and no business owner should have
to write compliance prose. You now just tick how customers actually reach you — they call, they text
first, they fill in a form — and NexPhone writes the reviewable description for you, publishes the exact
consent wording at a public link reviewers can check, and keeps every surface consistent automatically.

## Pre-submission checks that think like a reviewer

Before anything is submitted (and before any fee), NexPhone now:

- opens your **website** the way a reviewer will, and checks it actually shows your business name
- checks your **sample messages** for everything carriers reject — missing opt-out language, links,
  placeholders that nothing fills
- verifies your **consent pages** are reachable and carry the required notices
- shows you **exactly what the carriers will read**, word for word, before you pay

If something would fail, you find out in the wizard — not in a rejection email a week later.

## Status screens that tell the truth

Registration status is now a simple timeline: what's done, what's in review, and the one step (if any)
that needs you — with no phantom "enter your verification code" prompts after you've already verified.
If a registration is rejected, the screen shows the carrier's actual reason, points at the exact field
to fix, and "Fix & resubmit" reopens the form with everything filled in.

## Fair pricing on resubmissions

If a registration is rejected and your business verification survived, resubmitting charges only the
campaign-review portion (~$15), never the full fee again — and resubmitting *unchanged* material that
was rejected for content is refused outright, because paying twice for the same answer helps no one.

## Also this month

- Clear cost disclosure when picking a number: local vs. toll-free texting registration, side by side
- One-click "copy for Twilio support" — their team reviews drafted campaigns for free; now the paste is
  one button away
- Faster status updates across the board — approvals and rejections reflect within minutes
