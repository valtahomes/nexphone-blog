Before a US business can **send** text messages, carriers require a one-time registration — an anti-spam
rule that applies to every provider, not just us. What most sites bury in fine print: the process, the
cost, and the risk are very different depending on which kind of number you picked.

Receiving texts and answering calls need no registration at all. This is only about sending.

## Local numbers: A2P 10DLC registration

A local number (a regular area-code number) registers through **A2P 10DLC** — a system run by The
Campaign Registry (TCR) on behalf of the carriers.

- **~$19 in one-time carrier fees**, passed through at cost: ~$4 to verify your business, ~$15 for a
  third party to review your "campaign" — what you'll text and how customers consent.
- **The review is human and strict.** Reviewers open your website and check it matches your business
  name. They read your sample messages. They check how you collect consent, word by word.
- **A rejection costs real money.** Each re-review after a rejection is ~$15 — non-refundable, set by the
  registry. Neither we nor any provider can waive it.
- **One registration covers every local number** in your workspace, and it's permanent once approved.

## Toll-free numbers: free verification

A toll-free number (833, 844, 888…) uses a completely different process: **toll-free verification**.

- **Free to submit — and free to resubmit** if the reviewer asks for changes.
- Reviews check similar things (business identity, consent, message content), but a mistake costs you
  a week of waiting, not a fee.
- Verification is **per number** rather than per business.

## So which should you pick?

Be honest about what the number is for:

- **Calls first?** Pick local. Customers recognize local area codes and answer them; many people screen
  toll-free calls as telemarketing. For a service business whose phone is its storefront, this matters
  on every single call.
- **Texting first?** Pick toll-free. No registration fees, no per-attempt rejection cost, and identical
  capability once approved.
